## The Challenges of Writing Quality SOAP Notes

SOAP notes have been the cornerstone of medical documentation for decades. Comprised of Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan sections, SOAP notes capture key details from patient encounters to inform diagnosis and treatment. 

However, writing complete and compliant SOAP notes is time-consuming:

* **It takes up to 2 hours to write notes for a full day of patients.** Transcribing subjective patient reports, objective test findings, billing codes, and treatment plans is a manual, tedious process.
* **Time pressure leads to suboptimal notes.** Clinicians often delay documentation which can impact accuracy. Rushing to complete notes at the end of long shifts also increases errors.
* **Incomplete or inaccurate notes raise compliance risks.** Weak documentation can potentially violate reporting requirements and payer regulations if key details are missing.
